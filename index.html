FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your appointment with Lycia's Touch.

💬 Ask a Question
Do you travel to clients?
+
Yes! I offer home service throughout Lagos and nearby areas. A travel fee may apply depending on your location. Just let me know your address when booking and I'll provide all the details.
How much is bridal makeup?
+
Bridal makeup starts from ₦60,000 and varies based on the look complexity, location, and whether a trial session is included. Contact me via WhatsApp for a custom quote.
How do I book an appointment?
+
The easiest way is via WhatsApp! Click the "Book Now on WhatsApp" button, tell me the service you need, preferred date and time, and your location. I'll confirm availability and lock in your slot.
Do you offer makeup training?
+
Absolutely! I offer beginner, intermediate, and advanced makeup training programs. Classes are available in-person and include hands-on practice. Certificates are awarded upon completion.
What products do you use?
+
I use only high-quality, skin-friendly products from leading luxury makeup brands. All products are carefully selected to ensure long-lasting, flawless results for all skin types and tones.
How far in advance should I book?
+
For bridal makeup, I recommend booking at least 4–6 weeks in advance. For regular events and occasions, 1–2 weeks is usually sufficient. Weekends book fast, so reach out early!
