Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your appointment with Lycia's Touch.

💬 Ask a Question
Do you travel to clients?
+
Yes! I offer home service throughout Lagos and nearby areas. A travel fee may apply depending on your location. Just let me know your address when booking.
How much is bridal makeup?
+
Bridal makeup starts from ₦60,000 and varies based on complexity, location, and whether a trial is included. Contact me via WhatsApp for a custom quote.
How do I book an appointment?
+
The easiest way is via WhatsApp or using the booking calendar on this page. Tell me your service, preferred date and time, and your location. I'll confirm availability and lock in your slot.
Do you offer makeup training?
+
Absolutely! I offer beginner, intermediate, and advanced makeup training programs. Classes are in-person and include hands-on practice. Certificates are awarded upon completion.
What products do you use?
+
I use only high-quality, skin-friendly products from leading luxury makeup brands. All products are carefully selected for long-lasting, flawless results for all skin types and tones.
How far in advance should I book?
+
For bridal makeup, book at least 4–6 weeks in advance. For regular events, 1–2 weeks is usually sufficient. Weekends book fast, so reach out early!
Do you require a deposit?
+
Yes, a 50% deposit is required to confirm and secure your booking slot. The balance is due on the day of your appointment. Deposits are non-refundable for cancellations within 48 hours.
